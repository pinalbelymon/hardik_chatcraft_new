# ChatCraft App Store creative assets

English campaign: Your chats. Your stories.

- `chatcraft-header-3840x1646.png`: product page header, 3840 × 1646, RGB PNG.
- `chatcraft-search-3840x2560.png`: search results creative, 3840 × 2560, RGB PNG.

These are marketing creative assets, not app screenshots. No app source changes are required to use these files. Upload to the appropriate Header and Search Results fields in App Store Connect. Review the device-specific crop in App Store Connect Preview before submitting. These exports were resized from generated artwork to meet the placement dimensions.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. ExifTool removes removable embedded metadata from the final exports. This does not establish human authorship or guarantee that AI generation cannot be detected. Essential PNG format information, including pixel dimensions and color type, remains.

## Creative brief / prompt set

Header: Premium panoramic charcoal/electric-blue/mint campaign for ChatCraft, expressing conversations becoming stories with sculptural chat bubbles, typing dots and a subtle play motif. Exact copy: “ChatCraft” and “Your chats. Your stories.” Center key content, generous negative space, no other brands, awards, prices or download buttons.

Search: Premium 3:2 charcoal/electric-blue/mint chat-story campaign. Exact copy: “ChatCraft”, “Create chat stories”, “Write. Style. Play.” Two overlapping fictional conversation cards, blue/gray and mint/white. Sample dialogue: “I have a story idea.” / “Let’s bring it to life.” Center essential artwork, clear at thumbnail size, no phone hardware, no other brands, awards or pricing. Illustrative creative, not a screenshot of the shipping UI.
