# ChatCraft icon

Created using the built-in ImageGen tool. The old icon is preserved in `PreviousAppIcon`. The new full-resolution artwork is `chatcraft-icon-master.png`; production sizes are in the app's existing AppIcon asset catalog.

Design: electric blue full-bleed background, a white conversation bubble with a blue play cutout, and a small mint reply bubble. The mark connects chat creation with story playback, with fewer details for clarity at small sizes. The operating system applies the outer icon mask.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final production iOS app icon for ChatCraft, a fictional chat story maker where users write conversations and play them as stories. Create ONE exceptional finished square icon, 1024x1024 or larger square. Full bleed opaque electric cobalt-blue background (#086BFF, subtly darker toward lower edge). Center a bold, beautifully proportioned WHITE speech bubble with a clean right-pointing PLAY TRIANGLE cut out of its center, showing blue through it. The bubble body is a broad rounded conversational rectangle, with one elegant short integrated tail at lower left. The main white bubble occupies about 64% of canvas width, centered optically. A smaller mint reply bubble peeks out behind its upper-right shoulder, only one third exposed, communicating dialogue. Large confident geometry, immaculate curves, coherent consistent rounded forms, precise negative space. A distinctive crafted mark, not an emoji, not a stock messenger logo. Flat-first premium graphic design with a restrained subtle material highlight and minimal soft contact shadow only; NOT glossy inflated plastic or toy-like 3D. Clear silhouette and legibility at 29px. Generous 16% edge safety. No text, letters, wordmark, sparkles, stars, badges, outlines, tiny dots, decorative patterns, filmstrip, phone, extra symbols. Background extends to every edge. Do not bake rounded outer app-icon corners into the artwork; no outer frame, no white margin, no transparency. One icon only, no presentation sheet, no variants, no mockup.
